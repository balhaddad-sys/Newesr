OnCall Assistant
Evidence-Based Protocols
Clinical decision support only. Verify with local protocols. If unstable: activate emergency pathway.
Hypoglycemia
Glucose < 3.9 mmol/L
Urgent
Immediate Action
Conscious patient: 15-20g fast-acting glucose (juice, glucose tablets)
Unconscious/NPO: D50 25mL IV or Glucagon 1mg IM/SC
Monitoring
Recheck glucose in 15 minutes
Repeat treatment if still <4.0 mmol/L
Once stable: complex carbohydrate snack
Investigate cause (insulin dose, missed meal, illness)
Hyperkalemia
K+ > 6.0 mmol/L
Urgent
Step 1: Cardiac Stabilization
Calcium gluconate 10% 10mL IV over 2-3 min
Protects myocardium (does not lower K+)
May repeat if ECG changes persist
Step 2: Shift K+ Intracellularly
Insulin 10 units + D50 25mL IV
Salbutamol 10-20mg nebulized
Sodium bicarbonate 50mEq if acidotic
Step 3: Remove K+
Loop diuretics (if volume overloaded)
Kayexalate 30g PO/PR
Consider dialysis if refractory
Sepsis
Hour-1 Bundle
STAT
Within 1 Hour
1. Measure lactate - repeat if >2 mmol/L
2. Blood cultures before antibiotics
3. Broad-spectrum antibiotics
4. 30 mL/kg crystalloid for hypotension or lactate ≥4
5. Vasopressors if MAP <65 despite fluids
Reassess
Repeat focused exam (capillary refill, pulse, skin)
Check lactate clearance at 2-4 hours
Consider central access for vasopressors
Source control within 6-12 hours
Acute Heart Failure
Decompensated HF Management
Initial Assessment
ABC, vital signs, SpO2
12-lead ECG (ACS? Arrhythmia?)
CXR, BNP/NT-proBNP, troponin, renal function
Identify precipitant (infection, ACS, AF, non-adherence)
Treatment
Oxygen: Target SpO2 94-98%
Position: Sit upright
Diuresis: Furosemide 40-80mg IV (or 2x home dose)
If SBP >110: GTN infusion, consider CPAP
If SBP <90: Inotropes, consider ICU
COPD Exacerbation
Acute Management
Bronchodilators
Salbutamol 2.5-5mg neb Q20min x3, then Q4H
Ipratropium 500mcg neb Q4-6H
Consider IV salbutamol if severe
Additional Therapy
Steroids: Prednisone 40mg PO x 5 days
Antibiotics: If purulent sputum (Amox-clav or Doxycycline)
Oxygen: Target SpO2 88-92% (risk of CO2 retention)
NIV: If pH <7.35 and PaCO2 >45

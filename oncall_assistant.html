ICUCritical
Intensive Care Unit
Before Calling ICU
Airway: Patent? Protecting? GCS?
Breathing: RR, SpO2, O2 requirement, ABG
Circulation: HR, BP, MAP, lactate, access
Interventions: What you've done so far
ICU Admission Criteria
• Mechanical ventilation or imminent intubation
• Vasopressor requirement
• Severe sepsis/septic shock
• Active MI with hemodynamic instability
• Status epilepticus
• Acute renal failure requiring CRRT
• GCS ≤8 or rapidly declining
• Post-cardiac arrest
• DKA/HHS with pH <7.1 or altered mental status
📊 Must-Have Information
• Recent ABG with FiO2
• Lactate (ideally serial)
• Current vitals & trend over last 2-4 hours
• Fluid balance & urine output
• Recent labs: CBC, BMP, coags, LFTs
• ECG within last hour if cardiac concern
• Code status & goals of care
• Family contact information

Surgery
General Surgery
Before Calling Surgery
Localize: Where is the problem?
Imaging: CT/US done? Results?
NPO status: Last oral intake?
Anticoag: On blood thinners?
Required Information
• Chief complaint with timeline
• Physical exam: Peritonitis? Guarding? Rebound?
• Labs: CBC, BMP, lipase, LFTs, lactate, coags
• Imaging results (CT report or images available)
• Hemodynamic status
• Surgical history & previous abdominal surgeries
• Comorbidities affecting surgical risk
Urgent Surgical Conditions
• Perforated viscus / free air
• Acute appendicitis with peritonitis
• Small bowel obstruction with strangulation
• Acute mesenteric ischemia
• Necrotizing fasciitis
• Compartment syndrome
• Hemorrhage requiring operative control
• Acute cholecystitis (Tokyo Grade II-III)
Cardiology
Cardiac referral
STEMI - Activate Cath Lab
• Call cath lab FIRST - don't wait for consult
• ECG with ST elevation or new LBBB
• Symptom onset time (door-to-balloon <90 min)
• Aspirin 300mg + Ticagrelor 180mg given?
• Anticoagulation given?
• Contraindications to intervention?
Non-STEMI/Unstable Angina
• Serial ECGs (at least 2)
• Troponin at 0, 3, 6 hours
• GRACE/TIMI score if possible
• Current anti-ischemic therapy
• Bleeding risk assessment
• Echo findings if available
📊 Arrhythmia Consult
• 12-lead ECG (during arrhythmia if possible)
• Rhythm strip showing onset/termination
• Hemodynamic stability during episodes
• Electrolytes: K+, Mg2+, Ca2+
• Current medications (esp. QT-prolonging)
• Previous ablations or device history
Heart Failure Consult
• BNP/NT-proBNP level
• Echo with EF (recent or pending)
• Volume status: JVP, edema, crackles
• Daily weights & I/O balance
• Current diuretic regimen & response
• Renal function trend
Neurology
Neuro referral
Stroke Alert
• Time last seen normal - CRITICAL
• NIHSS score (or key deficits)
• CT head done? Hemorrhage ruled out?
• Glucose checked?
• BP - do NOT lower unless >220/120
• Anticoagulant use? (INR if on warfarin)
• Contraindications to tPA?
Seizure Consult
• Witnessed description of event
• Duration & post-ictal state
• First seizure vs known epilepsy
• AED levels if applicable
• Labs: glucose, Na+, Ca2+, Mg2+
• CT/MRI done?
• EEG ordered?
📊 Altered Mental Status
• GCS and trend
• Focal deficits? Pupils, motor exam
• Metabolic workup: glucose, Na+, ammonia, TSH
• Infectious workup: WBC, UA, CXR, blood Cx
• Toxicology screen if indicated
• CT head ± LP if meningitis suspected
Gastroenterology
GI referral
GI Bleed - Urgent
• Hemodynamic status: HR, BP, orthostatics
• Blood products: Type & Screen, transfusion needs
• Hgb/Hct trend
• Active bleeding? Hematemesis, melena, BRBPR
• Coags: INR, platelets
• Glasgow-Blatchford score
Liver/Pancreas Consult
• LFTs with trend
• Lipase (for pancreatitis)
• Imaging: US, CT, MRCP
• Hepatitis serologies if relevant
• INR, albumin (synthetic function)
• Ascites/encephalopathy status
Nephrology
Renal referral
Urgent Dialysis Indications (AEIOU)
• Acidosis - severe metabolic (pH <7.1)
• Electrolytes - K+ >6.5 refractory to medical Rx
• Ingestion - toxic alcohols, lithium, salicylates
• Overload - pulmonary edema refractory to diuretics
• Uremia - encephalopathy, pericarditis, bleeding
AKI Workup
• Baseline Cr & trend
• Urine output (hourly if available)
• UA with sediment
• FENa or FEUrea
• Renal US - hydronephrosis?
• Nephrotoxins review
• Volume status assessment
Pulmonology
Respiratory referral
Before Calling Pulm
O2 needs: FiO2, device, SpO2
ABG: With FiO2 at time of draw
CXR: Recent imaging findings
Work of breathing: RR, accessory muscles
Required Information
• Respiratory history: COPD, asthma, ILD, OSA
• Home O2? L/min, continuous vs nocturnal
• Recent PFTs if available
• CT chest findings
• Sputum culture results
• BiPAP/CPAP settings if applicable

Community-Acquired Pneumonia (CAP)
IDSA/ATS 2019
CURB-65 0-1, outpatient or low-risk inpatient
First Line
Amoxicillin 1g PO TDS
Or Doxycycline 100mg PO BD if atypical suspected
Penicillin Allergy
Doxycycline 100mg PO BD
Or Azithromycin 500mg Day 1, then 250mg OD x4 days
Duration: 5 days (minimum)
CAP - Moderate/Severe
IDSA/ATS 2019
CURB-65 ≥2, ward admission
First Line
Co-amoxiclav 1.2g IV TDS + Clarithromycin 500mg IV/PO BD
Add macrolide for atypical coverage
Penicillin Allergy
Levofloxacin 750mg IV/PO OD
Respiratory fluoroquinolone monotherapy
Duration: 5-7 days
Hospital-Acquired Pneumonia (HAP)
IDSA/ATS 2016
Pneumonia ≥48h after admission
First Line
Piperacillin-Tazobactam 4.5g IV Q6H
Or Cefepime 2g IV Q8H
Add if MRSA Risk
Vancomycin 15-20mg/kg IV Q8-12H
Target trough 15-20 mcg/mL
Duration: 7 days

Uncomplicated Cystitis
IDSA 2011
Women, no structural abnormalities
First Line
Nitrofurantoin 100mg PO BD x 5 days
Avoid if CrCl <30
Alternative
Trimethoprim 200mg PO BD x 3 days
If local resistance <20%
Pyelonephritis
IDSA 2011
Fever, flank pain, pyuria
Outpatient
Ciprofloxacin 500mg PO BD x 7 days
Or Trimethoprim-Sulfamethoxazole DS BD x 14 days
Inpatient
Ceftriaxone 1g IV OD
Or Ciprofloxacin 400mg IV BD
Duration: 7-14 days

Non-Purulent Cellulitis
IDSA 2014
Erythema, warmth, no abscess
Mild (Outpatient)
Flucloxacillin 500mg PO QDS
Or Cefalexin 500mg PO QDS
Moderate/Severe
Flucloxacillin 2g IV Q6H
Or Cefazolin 2g IV Q8H
Penicillin Allergy
Clindamycin 450mg PO TDS or 600mg IV Q8H
Duration: 5-7 days (can extend to 14 if slow response)
Purulent Cellulitis / Abscess
IDSA 2014
MRSA coverage required
First Line
Incision & Drainage + Trimethoprim-Sulfamethoxazole DS PO BD
Or Doxycycline 100mg PO BD
Severe
Vancomycin 15-20mg/kg IV Q8-12H
Target trough 15-20 mcg/mL

Acute Cholecystitis
Tokyo Guidelines 2018
Mild-Moderate
Ceftriaxone 2g IV OD + Metronidazole 500mg IV Q8H
Severe
Piperacillin-Tazobactam 4.5g IV Q6H
Or Meropenem 1g IV Q8H if critically ill
Acute Diverticulitis
AGA 2015
Uncomplicated
Ciprofloxacin 500mg PO BD + Metronidazole 400mg PO TDS
Or Amoxicillin-Clavulanate 875/125mg PO BD
Complicated (Inpatient)
Ceftriaxone 2g IV OD + Metronidazole 500mg IV Q8H
Duration: 4-7 days

Bacterial Meningitis - Empiric
IDSA 2017
Treat IMMEDIATELY - do not delay for imaging
Adults 18-50
Ceftriaxone 2g IV Q12H + Vancomycin 15-20mg/kg IV Q8-12H
Add Dexamethasone 0.15mg/kg IV Q6H x4 days (give before/with first antibiotic dose)
Age >50 or Immunocompromised
Add Ampicillin 2g IV Q4H
For Listeria coverage
Duration: 10-21 days (organism dependent)

Sepsis - Unknown Source
Surviving Sepsis 2021
Give within 1 hour of recognition
Community-Onset
Piperacillin-Tazobactam 4.5g IV Q6H
Or Ceftriaxone 2g IV OD + Metronidazole 500mg IV Q8H
Healthcare-Associated / Severe
Meropenem 1g IV Q8H + Vancomycin 15-20mg/kg IV Q8-12H
Consider Amikacin 15-20mg/kg IV OD if Pseudomonas risk
Beta-Lactam Allergy
Aztreonam 2g IV Q8H + Vancomycin + Metronidazole
